# Community Code of Conduct

AI Product Factory welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels and ideas.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, technical and constructive.

- critique code and architecture, not people
- explain trade-offs and evidence
- welcome questions and first contributions
- disclose limitations instead of overstating results
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats and personal attacks
- do not publish private information or credentials
- do not pressure contributors into unsafe or deceptive claims

## Technical disagreement

Strong disagreement is fine. Prefer reproducible examples, tests, benchmarks and documented constraints over status or volume.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove abusive content and may restrict participation for repeated or serious violations. Security reports should follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), not public escalation.

The goal is a community where people can build ambitious systems while keeping review, evidence and human control central.
